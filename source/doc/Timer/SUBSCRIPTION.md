### 2. Billing Rules

#### 2.1 Auto-Renewable Subscriptions (Monthly / Yearly)

- Initial subscription: upon purchase confirmation, the App Store will charge the first period to your payment method, and the subscription takes effect immediately
- Auto-renewal: within 24 hours before the end of each billing period, the App Store will automatically charge the next period to your payment method
- Failed renewal: if the charge fails (e.g., expired card, insufficient balance), the App Store will follow its established retry process; if all retries fail, the subscription will be terminated automatically
- Post-cancellation: if you cancel before the end of the current period, fees already paid are non-refundable; you can continue to use the subscription service until the end of the current period

#### 2.2 One-Time Purchase (Pro Lifetime)

- One-time charge: the full amount is charged to your payment method at purchase
- No renewal: no further charges after successful purchase, lifetime access
- Not affected by subscription price adjustments

### 3. Free Trial

The App currently does not offer a free trial. If a free trial is added in the future, the trial duration, cancellation rules, and other details will be clearly communicated via App Store notification and in-app prompt.

### 4. Cancellation and Refund

#### 4.1 Cancellation Path (Pro Monthly / Pro Yearly)

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → select "SnapTime" → Cancel Subscription

After cancellation, fees already paid for the current period will not be refunded on a pro-rata basis, but you may continue to use the subscription until the end of the current period. No further charges will occur.

#### 4.2 Refund Policy

- The App does not process refund requests directly
- All refund requests must be submitted through Apple App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
- Refund results are subject to Apple's review
- Pro Lifetime is generally not refundable, as the full content has been delivered

### 5. Upgrades and Downgrades

- Pro Monthly ↔ Pro Yearly: you may upgrade within the current subscription period via the App Store; price difference is calculated pro-rata by remaining days; downgrades take effect at the end of the current period
- Pro Lifetime and subscription tiers do not substitute each other: purchasing Pro Lifetime does not affect existing subscriptions; if both are held, the user's actual choice governs
