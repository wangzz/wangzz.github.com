# 悬浮时间 / SnapTime 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-07-18


## English Version

wangzz ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SnapTime (also displayed as 悬浮时间, hereinafter the "App") handles your information when you use it, and the rights you have over that information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

### 1. Information We Collect

#### 1.1 Information You Provide

- Shopping / flash-sale task configurations: task names, target links, scheduled times, and notes you create
- Time-scene presets: custom scene names, associated tasks, and styles
- App preferences: time format, skin selection, display mode, soundscape preferences, etc.
- Feedback: opinions, problem descriptions, and optional screenshots you submit via the in-app feedback channel

#### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- Device basics: device model, iOS version, app version (used only for compatibility adaptation and local crash diagnostics)
- Local app state: shopping task execution results, Win Index history (stored locally only, used to provide trend insights)
- Subscription receipts: stored only in the device's local Keychain, used for purchase restoration and entitlement verification

#### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

The App explicitly does NOT collect:

- Any identity information (name, phone number, ID number, email, Apple ID password, etc.)
- Your precise or coarse geographic location
- Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
- Your call logs, SMS, or browsing history
- Any user behavior profiles, advertising identifiers (IDFA) tracking data
- Any cross-app data used for ad targeting

### 2. How We Use the Information

We use the above information only to the extent necessary to provide the App's core features:

- Render the floating time, Live Activity, and desktop picture-in-picture clock with high precision
- Orchestrate multi-step flash-sale flows, performing local operations such as link pre-warming and vibration countdown at preset times
- Record your local task history, Win Index, and annual report for review and strategy optimization
- Sync your settings to widgets, Live Activities, and the picture-in-picture surface for consistent multi-surface display
- Handle the feedback and questions you actively submit

All processing is completed on your device locally. We do not rely on any backend service.

### 3. Storage and Sync

- All tasks, settings, and historical data are stored in your device's local sandbox
- iCloud sync (only when you explicitly enable it) is transmitted via Apple's end-to-end encrypted channel; the developer cannot access the data
- Subscription receipts are stored only in the device's local Keychain and are not uploaded to any server

### 4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

- We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information with any third party
- We do not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDK
- If future business changes require adding new third-party data processing, we will clearly state it in the updated Privacy Policy and re-obtain your consent

### 5. Your Rights

You have the following rights over your personal information:

- Right of access: you can view, edit, and delete your tasks, settings, and history in the App at any time
- Right of deletion: you can clear all local data with one click via "Me - Settings - Clear All Data"
- Right to withdraw consent: you can disable notifications, Live Activities, ATT authorization, and any other granted permission in iOS Settings
- Right of account deletion: the App does not maintain an account system, so this right is naturally satisfied

### 6. Third-Party SDKs

The App does not integrate any third-party SDK, including but not limited to:

- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- Umeng Analytics / Push / Share
- Firebase / Crashlytics
- Any social-sharing, third-party payment, or third-party login SDK

In-app jumps to "JD / Taobao / Xiaohongshu / 12306 / Alipay" etc. are performed via the standard iOS `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes` mechanism, handing off to the target App which processes its own data independently. The App has no access to the data handled by those third-party Apps.

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a guardian of a minor and discover that a minor has submitted personal information without your consent, please contact us using the details below and we will delete the relevant information immediately.

### 8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy to reflect product feature changes, legal/regulatory changes, or other circumstances. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app prompt or the version update notes. Continued use of the App constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
