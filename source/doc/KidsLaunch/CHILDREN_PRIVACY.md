# 儿童启蒙乐园 儿童隐私政策

**生效日期：** 2026-08-27

**开发者：** wangzz

**联系邮箱：** wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com

**地址：** 省略地址

## 1. 我们的承诺

《儿童启蒙乐园》是一款面向 4 岁以上儿童的启蒙教育应用。我们高度重视儿童隐私，并依据《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》、COPPA（美国儿童在线隐私保护规则）等相关法律法规制定本儿童隐私政策。

## 2. 我们不收集未成年人的个人信息

本应用**不运行任何后端服务器，也不将任何数据上传至任何服务器**。因此，我们**不收集、不存储、不传输**任何未成年人的个人信息，包括：

- 不收集姓名、年龄、学校等身份信息；
- 不收集设备标识符、广告标识符（IDFA）；
- 不收集位置、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风等敏感信息；
- 不收集任何行为分析或追踪数据。

## 3. 监护人同意

本应用**不会在未经监护人同意的情况下收集任何未成年人的个人信息**。如未来任何功能需要收集儿童信息，我们将：

- 事先向监护人明确告知收集目的与范围；
- 取得可核实的监护人同意；
- 仅将所收集信息用于经同意的目的，并提供监护人查阅、更正与删除的渠道。

## 4. 第三方

本应用仅集成 Apple 的 StoreKit 用于应用内购买，由 Apple 处理交易信息，我们不会获取儿童的支付信息。除 StoreKit 外不集成任何第三方 SDK。

## 5. 联系我们

如您对本儿童隐私政策有疑问或需行使权利，请联系：wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com（地址：省略地址）。

# KidsLaunch Children's Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-27

**Developer:** wangzz

**Contact Email:** wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com

**Address:** 省略地址

**Jurisdiction:** China

## 1. Our Commitment

KidsLaunch is an educational app for children aged 4 and above. We take children's privacy seriously and have adopted this Children's Privacy Policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of China, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule), and other applicable regulations.

## 2. We Do Not Collect Minors' Personal Information

The App **does not operate any backend server and does not upload any data to any server**. Therefore, we **do not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information of minors, including:

- No identity information such as name, age, or school;
- No device identifiers or Advertising Identifier (IDFA);
- No sensitive information such as location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone;
- No behavioral analytics or tracking data.

## 3. Parental Consent

The App **will not collect any personal information of minors without parental consent**. Should any future feature require collecting children's information, we will:

- Clearly inform the parent of the purpose and scope in advance;
- Obtain verifiable parental consent;
- Use the collected information only for the consented purpose, and provide parents with access, correction, and deletion channels.

## 4. Third Parties

The App integrates only Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases; Apple processes transaction information and we never receive children's payment details. No other third-party SDKs are integrated.

If you have questions about this Children's Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, contact: wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com (Address: 省略地址).
