## 1. 概述

本应用通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 提供以下应用内购买项目：

- **会员 月订阅**：¥18 / 月，自动续期。
- **会员 年订阅**：¥38 / 年，自动续期；新订阅用户享 **3 天免费试用**。
- **终身版**：¥88 一次性买断（非消耗型）。

订阅组名称：儿童启蒙乐园 会员（KidsLaunch Premium）。月订阅与年订阅属于同一订阅组，同一 Apple ID 在同一时间只能选择其中一个档位。

## 2. 自动续期说明

订阅（月订阅、年订阅）为**自动续期**项目。在当前订阅周期结束前至少 24 小时，您的订阅将**自动续期**，并从您的 Apple ID 账户扣取下一周期费用。

## 3. 续期价格与订阅周期

- 会员 月订阅：¥18 / 月，订阅周期为 1 个月。
- 会员 年订阅：¥38 / 年，订阅周期为 1 年（新用户含 3 天免费试用，试用期内取消不收费）。
- 终身版：¥88，一次性支付，永久解锁，不续期。

上述价格为中国区价格，其他地区以 App Store 展示的当地货币价格为准。

## 4. 免费试用

年订阅为首次订阅的新用户提供 **3 天免费试用**。免费试用将在到期时自动转为付费订阅，除非您在试用结束前至少 24 小时取消。试用期内取消不收取任何费用。每个 Apple ID 仅可享受一次试用期。

## 5. 如何管理或取消

打开 iOS **设置 → [您的姓名] → 订阅**，选择"儿童启蒙乐园"并点击"取消订阅"。

终身版为一次性购买，不存在续期，无需取消。

## 6. 家庭共享

月订阅、年订阅与终身版均支持**家庭共享**。开启后，同一"家人共享"组内的成员可共享已解锁的全部内容，无需重复购买。

## 7. 恢复购买

更换设备或重装应用后，可在应用内 **设置 → 恢复购买**，通过 Apple ID 免费恢复此前已购买的全部权益，不会重复扣费。

## 1. Overview

The App offers the following in-app purchases through Apple's StoreKit:

- **Premium Monthly:** CNY 18 / month, auto-renewable.
- **Premium Yearly:** CNY 38 / year, auto-renewable; new subscribers get a **3-day free trial**.
- **Lifetime:** CNY 88, one-time non-consumable purchase.

Subscription group: KidsLaunch Premium. The Monthly and Yearly plans belong to the same subscription group, so one Apple ID can hold only one of them at a time.

## 2. Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) are **auto-renewable**. Your subscription will **automatically renew** at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and the next period's fee will be charged to your Apple ID account.

## 3. Renewal Price and Subscription Period

- Premium Monthly: CNY 18 per month; subscription period is 1 month.
- Premium Yearly: CNY 38 per year; subscription period is 1 year (new subscribers include a 3-day free trial; canceling during the trial incurs no charge).
- Lifetime: CNY 88, one-time payment, permanently unlocked, no renewal.

Prices above apply to the China storefront. Other storefronts display the equivalent price in local currency.

## 4. Free Trial

The Yearly plan offers new first-time subscribers a **3-day free trial**. The trial automatically converts to a paid subscription at expiry unless canceled at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Canceling during the trial incurs no charge. Each Apple ID may redeem the trial only once.

## 5. How to Manage or Cancel

You may cancel at any time; cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current paid period, and benefits for the paid period remain unaffected. **To cancel:**

Open iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**, select "KidsLaunch", and tap "Cancel Subscription".

Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no renewal, so no cancellation is needed.

## 6. Family Sharing

The Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime options all support **Family Sharing**. Once enabled, members of the same Family Sharing group can share all unlocked content without purchasing again.

## 7. Restore Purchases

After switching devices or reinstalling the App, open **Settings → Restore Purchases** to restore all previously purchased entitlements through your Apple ID at no additional charge.

## 8. Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. Apple **does not support developer-direct refunds**. For a refund, please use Apple's official channel (reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple will handle it under its policies.

## 9. Free and Paid Content

The first level of every subject is permanently free and can be played without any purchase. A subscription or lifetime unlock grants access to all 37 levels across 7 subjects.

## 10. Price Changes

Apple will notify you in advance and obtain your consent (where applicable) before any price increase takes effect.
