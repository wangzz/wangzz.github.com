**适用管辖：** 中国

## 1. 概述

本应用通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 提供以下应用内购买项目：

- **周订阅**：¥12 / 周，自动续期。
- **年订阅**：¥128 / 年，自动续期；新订阅用户享 3 天免费试用。
- **终身买断**：¥38 一次性买断（非消耗型）。

## 2. 自动续期说明

订阅（周订阅、年订阅）为**自动续期**项目。在当前订阅周期结束前，您的订阅将**自动续期**，并从您的 Apple ID 账户扣取下一周期费用。

## 3. 续期价格与订阅周期

- 周订阅：¥12 / 周，订阅周期为 1 周。
- 年订阅：¥128 / 年，订阅周期为 1 年（新用户含 3 天免费试用，试用期内取消不收费）。
- 终身买断：¥38，一次性支付，永久解锁，不续期。

## 4. 免费试用

年订阅为首次订阅的新用户提供 3 天免费试用。免费试用将在到期时自动转为付费订阅，除非您在试用结束前取消。

## 5. 如何取消

您可以随时取消订阅，取消后将在当前已付费周期结束时停止续期。**取消方式：**

打开 iOS **设置 → [您的姓名] → 订阅**，选择"儿童启蒙乐园"并点击"取消订阅"。

## 6. 退款

所有购买均通过 Apple 完成。Apple **不支持开发者直接退款**；如您需要退款，请通过 Apple 的官方渠道（报告问题：reportaproblem.apple.com）申请，由 Apple 按相关政策处理。

## 7. 价格调整

Apple 会在任何价格上涨生效前至少提前通知您，并取得您的同意（如适用）。

# KidsLaunch Subscription Terms

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-27

**Developer:** wangzz

**Contact Email:** wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com

**Address:** 省略地址

**Jurisdiction:** China

## 1. Overview

The App offers the following in-app purchases through Apple's StoreKit:

- **Weekly Subscription:** CNY 12 / week, auto-renewable.
- **Yearly Subscription:** CNY 128 / year, auto-renewable; new subscribers get a 3-day free trial.
- **Lifetime:** CNY 38, one-time non-consumable purchase.

## 2. Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions (Weekly and Yearly) are **auto-renewable**. Your subscription will **automatically renew** before the current period ends and the next period's fee will be charged to your Apple ID account.

## 3. Renewal Price and Subscription Period

- Weekly Subscription: CNY 12 per week; subscription period is 1 week.
- Yearly Subscription: CNY 128 per year; subscription period is 1 year (new subscribers include a 3-day free trial; canceling during the trial incurs no charge).
- Lifetime: CNY 38, one-time payment, permanently unlocked, no renewal.

## 4. Free Trial

The Yearly Subscription offers new first-time subscribers a 3-day free trial. The trial automatically converts to a paid subscription at expiry unless canceled before the trial ends.

## 5. How to Cancel

You may cancel at any time; cancellation stops renewal at the end of the current paid period. **To cancel:**

Open iOS **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**, select "KidsLaunch", and tap "Cancel Subscription".

## 6. Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. Apple **does not support developer-direct refunds**. For a refund, please use Apple's official channel (reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple will handle it under its policies.

## 7. Price Changes

Apple will notify you in advance and obtain your consent (where applicable) before any price increase takes effect.
