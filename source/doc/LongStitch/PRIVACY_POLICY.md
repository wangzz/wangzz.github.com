# 拼长图 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-09-08

## 中文版

wangzz（以下简称"我们"）高度重视用户隐私保护。本政策说明我们在您使用 拼长图（以下简称"本应用"）时如何收集、使用、存储、共享您的个人信息，以及您对这些信息的控制方式。请您在使用本应用前仔细阅读本政策。

### 1. 我们收集的信息

#### 1.1 您主动提供的信息

本应用无账号体系，不要求注册。您主动提供的信息仅限：通过「设置 → 反馈建议」邮件入口发送给我们的反馈内容。该邮件由您的邮件客户端直接发送至我们的邮箱，不经过任何由我们运营的服务器。

#### 1.2 自动收集的信息

- 无。本应用不自动收集任何个人信息。所有图片处理（拼接、编辑、导出）、历史记录与功能日志均仅保存在您的设备本地，不上传任何服务器。

#### 1.3 我们不会收集的信息

本应用不会收集以下信息：
- 精确地理位置
- 通讯录
- 健康数据
- 照片内容不会离开您的设备：本应用对相册图片的全部处理（长图拼接、遮挡、标注、导出）均在设备本地完成

### 2. 我们如何使用这些信息

- 向您提供本应用的核心功能（长图拼接、编辑与导出）
- 响应您主动发送的咨询与反馈
- 遵守适用的法律法规

我们不会将您的个人信息用于上述目的之外的用途，也不会向第三方出售您的个人信息。

### 3. 第三方服务

本应用集成了以下第三方服务，这些服务可能会收集必要的信息用于其声明的用途：

- StoreKit（Apple，应用内购买系统组件；V1.0 起通过 Apple 内购系统销售订阅与买断商品，购买、续期与退款均由 Apple 处理，本应用不接触支付信息）

我们已与上述第三方签署数据处理协议，要求其按照不低于本政策的标准处理您的信息。

### 4. 信息存储与安全

- 存储地点：您的全部数据（长图结果、历史记录、编辑草稿、使用偏好）仅存储在您的设备本地，无云端同步，无服务器备份
- 存储期限：本地数据保存至您删除为止（卸载本应用即删除全部本地数据；历史记录可在应用内「历史」页单条删除）
- 安全措施：我们采用访问控制等技术措施保护您的信息；由于数据不出设备，不存在传输环节风险

### 5. 您的权利

您有权：

- 访问和查看您的本地数据（历史记录、编辑草稿）
- 删除您的数据（应用内「历史」页单条删除，或卸载本应用删除全部数据）
- 撤回您先前授予的权限（在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 拼长图」中调整相册权限）

### 6. 未成年人保护

本应用的目标用户不包含未满 14 周岁的未成年人。如果您是未满 14 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人同意并陪同下使用本应用。我们不会在未获得监护人同意的情况下主动收集未成年人的个人信息。

### 7. 政策更新

我们可能会不定期更新本政策。更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并修改生效日期。重大变更我们会通过应用内通知或其他方式提示您。

如您对本政策有任何疑问、意见或投诉，请通过以下方式联系我们：

## English Version

wangzz ("we", "us", "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use 拼长图 (the "App"), and the choices you have regarding that information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

### 1. Information We Collect

#### 1.1 Information You Provide

The App has no account system and requires no registration. The only information you may provide is feedback you choose to send us via the "Settings → Feedback" email link. That email is sent directly from your mail client to our inbox and never passes through any server operated by us.

#### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- None. The App does not automatically collect any personal information. All image processing (stitching, editing, exporting), history records, and function logs are stored locally on your device and never uploaded to any server.

#### 1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect:
- Precise location
- Contacts
- Health data
- Your photos never leave your device: all processing of library images (stitching, masking, annotating, exporting) happens locally on the device

### 2. How We Use Information

- To provide the core functionality of the App (stitching, editing, and exporting long images)
- To respond to inquiries and feedback you proactively send us
- To comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not use your personal information for purposes beyond those stated above, nor do we sell your personal information to third parties.

### 3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services. These services may collect information necessary to fulfill their stated purposes:

- StoreKit (Apple, in-app purchase system component; since V1.0, subscription and one-time purchase products are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system, with billing, renewal and refunds handled by Apple; the App does not handle payment information)

We have signed data processing agreements with these parties requiring them to handle your information at a standard no less strict than this policy.

### 4. Storage and Security

- Location: All of your data (stitched results, history records, edit drafts, preferences) is stored locally on your device, with no cloud sync and no server backup
- Retention: Local data is kept until you delete it (uninstalling the App removes all local data; history entries can be deleted individually on the History page)
- Safeguards: We use access controls to protect your information; since data never leaves the device, there is no transmission risk

### 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access and review your local data (history records, edit drafts)
- Delete your data (individual entries on the in-app History page, or all data by uninstalling the App)
- Withdraw previously granted permissions (via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → 拼长图)

### 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the App only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

### 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date. We will notify you of material changes through in-app notifications or other means.

If you have any questions, comments, or complaints about this policy, please contact:

- Address: 省略地址
