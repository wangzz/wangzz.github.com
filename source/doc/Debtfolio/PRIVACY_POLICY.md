# 理债 Debtfolio 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-23

开发者：wangzz
联系邮箱：wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com
适用法律：中华人民共和国法律

## 1. 概述

理债（英文名 Debtfolio，以下简称"本应用"）是一款在设备本地运行的贷款组合管理工具。本应用没有账号体系、没有服务器后端、不接入任何广告或统计分析服务。您在应用内录入的全部内容仅保存在您自己的设备上。

## 2. 我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息，具体包括：

- 不收集联系方式（姓名、邮箱、电话、地址）
- 不收集位置信息
- 不收集设备标识符（IDFA、IDFV 均不读取）
- 不收集使用行为、崩溃诊断或性能数据
- 不收集您在应用内录入的贷款、房产、家庭信息

## 3. 您录入数据的存储方式

- 存储位置：您录入的贷款档案、还款计划与模拟结果通过系统提供的 SwiftData 框架保存在本机应用沙盒内
- 是否上传：否。本应用不具备联网上传能力，数据不会离开您的设备
- 是否云同步：否。当前版本不启用 iCloud 同步
- 保存期限：直到您在应用内删除该条记录，或卸载本应用

## 4. 系统权限

本应用仅可能请求以下一项系统授权：

- 通知权限：用于在贷款扣款日前发送本地提醒。提醒由设备本地生成，不使用远程推送。拒绝授权后，日历与测算功能仍可正常使用

本应用不申请定位、相机、相册、通讯录、麦克风、健康等任何其他敏感权限。

## 5. 第三方服务

本应用未接入任何第三方广告、统计、追踪或数据分析 SDK。涉及的外部服务仅有一项：

- Apple StoreKit：用于处理应用内购买与订阅。支付与交易凭证由 Apple 处理，开发者无法获取您的支付信息

## 6. 数据导出与删除

- 导出：您可在应用内将贷款清单导出为 CSV、将年度本息报告导出为 PDF，文件由您自行决定保存位置或分享对象
- 删除单条记录：在应用内对应页面删除
- 删除全部数据：卸载本应用即可清除全部本地数据。由于没有服务端副本，无需额外申请删除

## 7. 儿童隐私

本应用面向具备完全民事行为能力的成年用户，不面向儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

## 8. 政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新生效日期与内容。继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的政策。

## 9. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至 wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com，或通过应用内"设置 - 反馈"联系我们。

# Debtfolio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-10-23

Developer: wangzz
Contact: wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com
Governing Law: Laws of the People's Republic of China

## 1. Overview

Debtfolio is an on-device loan portfolio management tool. The app has no account system, no backend server, and integrates no advertising or analytics services. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

- No contact details (name, email, phone, address)
- No location data
- No device identifiers (we do not read IDFA or IDFV)
- No usage analytics, crash diagnostics, or performance data
- No loan, property, or household data you enter in the app

## 3. How Your Data Is Stored

- Location: loan records, repayment schedules and simulation results are stored in the app sandbox on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
- Upload: none. The app has no capability to upload data to any server
- Cloud sync: none. iCloud sync is not enabled in this version
- Retention: until you delete a record in the app, or uninstall the app

## 4. System Permissions

The app may request only one system permission:

- Notifications: used to deliver local reminders before a loan payment due date. Reminders are generated on the device and no remote push is used. If you decline, the calendar and calculation features still work normally

The app does not request location, camera, photo library, contacts, microphone, health, or any other sensitive permission.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or data-processing SDKs. The only external service involved is:

- Apple StoreKit: used to process in-app purchases and subscriptions. Payments and receipts are handled by Apple; the developer cannot access your payment information

## 6. Data Export and Deletion

- Export: you may export your loan list as CSV or an annual principal and interest report as PDF, and choose where to save or share the file
- Delete a record: delete it on the relevant screen in the app
- Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all local data. There is no server-side copy, so no additional deletion request is needed

## 7. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for adult users who can enter into binding contracts. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and content on this page. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com or use Settings - Feedback inside the app.
