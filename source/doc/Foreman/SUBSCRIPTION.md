## 中文版

本《订阅条款》是《用户协议》的组成部分，适用于您通过 App Store 购买的 Foreman Pro 订阅与一次性商品。请在购买前仔细阅读。

### 1. 订阅项目

Foreman Pro 提供以下商品：

- 月度订阅：订阅周期为 1 个月，自动续期
- 年度订阅：订阅周期为 1 年，自动续期；新订阅用户可享 3 天免费试用
- 终身会员：一次性购买，非消耗型商品，无需续期与取消

各商品在您所在国家或地区的具体价格、税费与显示币种，以购买时 App Store 页面显示为准。

### 2. Pro 权益

订阅或购买后解锁：不限次聊天、按任务切换模型、模拟器实时预览与触控回灌、远程桌面、配对多台 Mac、远程构建与安装。推送提醒对所有用户开放，不属于订阅权益。

Pro 权益与具体功能可能随版本迭代调整；调整不会削减您在当前付费周期内已获得的权益。

### 3. 免费试用

年度订阅的新订阅用户可享 3 天免费试用。免费试用资格由 Apple 按 Apple ID 与订阅组判定，同一订阅组仅可享受一次。请在试用期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则将按年度订阅价格自动扣费。免费试用期内取消不会收费。

### 4. 自动续期与扣费

订阅将通过您的 Apple ID 账户扣费，并在每个订阅周期结束前 24 小时内自动续期，续期成功后有效期延长一个周期。若 Apple 无法完成扣费，订阅可能终止。

### 5. 取消订阅

您可以随时在 Apple ID 的订阅管理页取消订阅：iOS「设置」→ 您的 Apple ID →「订阅」→ 选择 Foreman →「取消订阅」。取消后，您仍可使用至当前订阅周期结束，已支付费用不予退还。

### 6. 恢复购买

在新设备或重装应用后，可在应用内「设置」或付费墙点击「恢复购买」恢复已购权益。

### 7. 退款

所有购买与退款均由 Apple 处理。如需退款，请通过 Apple 的退款流程提交申请；我们无法直接为您办理退款。

### 8. 价格调整

我们可能调整商品价格。价格调整不适用于您已生效的当前订阅周期，并会按 Apple 的规则提前通知。

### 1. Products

Foreman Pro offers the following products:

- Monthly subscription: 1-month term, auto-renewing

A subscription or purchase unlocks unlimited chat, per-task model selection, live simulator preview with touch injection, remote desktop, pairing with multiple Macs, and remote build and install. Push notifications are available to all users and are not a subscription benefit.

Pro entitlements and specific features may change as the App evolves; such changes will not remove entitlements already obtained during your current paid period.

### 3. Free Trial

New yearly subscribers may receive a 3-day free trial. Eligibility is determined by Apple based on your Apple ID and the subscription group, and is available once per subscription group. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, otherwise the yearly subscription price will be charged automatically. Cancelling during the free trial incurs no charge.

### 4. Auto-Renewal and Billing

Subscriptions are charged to your Apple ID account and renew automatically within 24 hours before the end of each period; a successful renewal extends access by one period. If Apple cannot charge your account, the subscription may end.

You may cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings: iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Foreman → Cancel Subscription. After cancelling, you can continue to use Pro until the end of the current period. Payments already made are non-refundable.

### 6. Restore Purchases

After reinstalling the App or switching devices, tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the paywall to restore your entitlements.

All purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use Apple's refund process; we cannot issue refunds directly.

### 8. Price Changes

We may change product prices. Price changes do not apply to your current active subscription period and will be communicated in advance in accordance with Apple's rules.
