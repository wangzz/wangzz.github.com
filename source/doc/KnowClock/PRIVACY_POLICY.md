# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — 认识时钟

**生效日期 / Effective Date：2026-10-24**

---

## 中文版

KnowClock（认识时钟，以下简称"本应用"）由开发者 wangzz（以下简称"我们"）提供。本应用面向 2-8 岁儿童及其家长，我们高度重视儿童隐私保护。本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理您与孩子的信息。

### 1. 我们处理的信息

**1.1 存储在您设备本地的信息**

- 学习数据：关卡进度、答题正确率、能力测评记录、时间训练成绩
- 习惯数据：每日使用时长、连续学习天数、作息打卡记录
- 设置数据：儿童年龄档位、家长设置的时长锁、通知开关
- 购买信息：订阅与买断状态，通过 App Store 处理

以上数据全部保存在您设备的本地存储中，**不会上传至任何服务器**。本应用没有后端服务。

**1.2 我们不收集的信息**

- 不收集儿童的姓名、生日、照片、声音或其他个人身份信息
- 不收集地理位置信息
- 不收集通讯录、相册内容或社交关系
- 不要求注册账号，无需登录
- 不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不接入任何广告或统计分析 SDK
- 不使用任何第三方 SDK

### 2. 信息的使用

本地存储的信息仅用于以下目的：

- 记录学习进度，下次打开时继续
- 生成本地的能力测评报告与成长周报
- 执行家长设置的每日时长锁
- 按家长设置的作息节点发送本地提醒

我们不会将任何信息用于广告投放，也不会向第三方出售或分享任何信息。

### 3. 权限说明

- **通知**：仅在家长主动开启作息提醒或试用到期提醒时申请，用于在本机发出提醒，不涉及任何网络传输
- **相册（仅写入）**：仅在家长主动点击"保存到相册"导出成长周报或练习纸时使用，本应用不读取您的相册内容

### 4. 儿童隐私（COPPA / GDPR-Kids）

- 本应用无需账号，儿童不会产生可识别身份的在线数据
- 所有涉及付费、修改设置、重置进度的入口均由家长门（数学验证题）保护
- 家长可随时在应用内重置全部学习数据

### 5. 第三方链接

应用内可能展示开发者其他教育类应用的推荐。相关推荐由开发者本人制作，指向 App Store 官方页面，点击后会离开本应用。除此之外，本应用不含任何外部网页链接、社交分享 SDK 或应用内浏览器。

### 6. 数据保留与删除

所有数据仅存在于本机。卸载应用即彻底删除全部学习数据与设置，且无法恢复。家长也可通过应用内重置进度清除。

### 7. 政策更新

如本政策更新，我们会在新版本中更新生效日期。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

### 8. 联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请通过 App Store 应用页面列出的支持网址与我们联系。

## English

KnowClock ("the App") is provided by developer wangzz ("we"). The App is designed for children aged 2-8 and their parents. This policy explains how the App handles information.

### 1. Information We Handle

**1.1 Stored locally on your device**

- Learning data: level progress, quiz accuracy, skill assessment records, time-sense training results
- Habit data: daily usage duration, learning streak, routine check-ins
- Settings: child's age bracket, parent-set time limit, notification preferences
- Purchase data: subscription and lifetime unlock status, processed by the App Store

All of the above is stored locally on your device and **never uploaded to any server**. The App has no backend.

**1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect**

- No child's name, birthday, photo, voice, or other personally identifiable information
- No location data
- No contacts, photo library content, or social graph
- No account registration or sign-in required
- No advertising identifier (IDFA); no advertising or analytics SDK
- No third-party SDK of any kind

### 2. How Information Is Used

Locally stored data is used only to:

- Resume learning progress on the next launch
- Generate on-device skill assessment reports and weekly growth reports
- Enforce the parent-set daily time limit
- Deliver local notifications for parent-configured routines

We never use this information for advertising and never sell or share it with third parties.

### 3. Permissions

- **Notifications**: requested only when a parent enables routine reminders or trial-ending reminders. Delivered on-device; no network transmission involved.
- **Photo Library (add-only)**: used only when a parent taps "Save to Photos" to export a weekly report or worksheet. The App does **not read** your photo library.

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-Kids)

- No account is required; children generate no identifiable online data
- All purchase, settings, and progress-reset entry points are guarded by a parental gate (math challenge)
- Parents can reset all learning data at any time inside the App

### 5. Third-Party Links

The App may display recommendations for the developer's other educational apps. These are created by the developer and point to official App Store pages; tapping one leaves the App. Apart from this, the App contains no external web links, social sharing SDKs, or in-app browser.

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

All data exists only on the device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all learning data and settings, which cannot be recovered. Parents may also clear data via "Reset Progress" in the App.

### 7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date in a new version. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

### 8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach us through the support URL listed on the App Store page.
