需要帮助？下面是常见问题，看一遍通常就解决了。没解决请发邮件到 wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com，我们会在两个工作日内回复。

判读结果不准 / 想看更多维度

判读结果由判读模型给出，属于参考性判断。结果由「意图、情绪倾向、敷衍倾向」三部分组成，每种倾向都有概率与置信度档位；置信度显示为「低」时，说明输入信息不足，建议换一段更完整的对话再试。

未订阅时为什么不能判读

意读的判读、历史记录、态度趋势三类功能都需要有效的订阅或买断权益，这是当前的产品设定。开通后不限次数使用，并解锁追问。

如何恢复购买

在 App 内进入「设置 → 恢复购买」，用购买时同一个 Apple ID 即可恢复。更换设备或重装应用后同样适用。

如何取消订阅 / 管理订阅

iOS 设置 → 顶部您的 Apple ID 头像 → 订阅 → 选择意读 → 取消订阅。

也可以直接访问 https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions 。

年订阅的 3 天免费试用期内取消不产生费用，详细条款见《订阅条款》。

如何删除我的数据

历史记录默认关闭。开启后仅保存在本机，可在「设置」中单条或全部删除；删除后无法恢复。卸载应用会清除本机全部数据。我们侧不保存任何对话内容。

我可以用自己的 API Key 吗

可以。在「设置」中填入你自己的 Jev API Key（BYOK）后，判读请求会直连 Jev 官方服务，使用你自己的账号与配额。

隐私相关

我们不做服务器中转，截图文字识别全部在本机完成，图片不会上传。对话文本仅用于当次判读，不留存、不出售、不用于训练。完整说明见《隐私政策》。

反馈建议

App 内「设置 → 反馈建议」可直接发起反馈。也请附上对应的对话文本（可自行打码）与截图，方便我们复现。

English Version

Need help? The questions below cover most issues. If yours is not here, email wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com and we will reply within two business days.

The judgment looks off / I want more detail

Results come from the judgment model and are advisory. Every result covers three dimensions — intent, emotional leaning, and perfunctory tendency — each with a probability and a confidence band. When confidence reads “low”, the input was probably too thin: try a longer, more complete conversation.

Why can I not judge without subscribing

Judging, history, and attitude trends all require a valid subscription or lifetime purchase. This is the product’s current design. Once active, judgments are unlimited and follow-ups unlock.

How do I restore a purchase

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the App and sign in with the same Apple ID used at purchase time. This also works after switching devices or reinstalling.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription

iOS Settings → your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → select IntentRead → Cancel.

You can also open https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions directly.

Cancelling during the 3-day free trial of the yearly plan incurs no charge. See the Subscription Terms for details.

How do I delete my data

History is off by default. When enabled it stays on your device and can be deleted individually or in bulk from Settings; deletion is permanent. Uninstalling the App clears everything stored locally. We keep no copy of your conversations.

Can I use my own API key

Yes. Enter your own Jev API key under Settings (BYOK) and requests go directly to Jev’s official service under your own account and quota.

Privacy

There is no server of ours in the path: screenshot recognition happens entirely on your device and images are never uploaded. Conversation text is used only for the judgment you requested, and is never stored, sold, or used for training. See the Privacy Policy for the full description.

Feedback

Use Settings → Feedback inside the App, or email us directly. Including the conversation text (masked if you prefer) and a screenshot helps us reproduce the issue.
