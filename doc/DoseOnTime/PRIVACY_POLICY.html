中文版

wangzz（以下简称”我们”）高度重视用户隐私保护。本政策说明我们在您使用 到点吃药（以下简称”本应用”）时如何收集、使用、存储、共享您的个人信息，以及您对这些信息的控制方式。请您在使用本应用前仔细阅读本政策。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 您主动提供的信息

本应用无账号体系，无需注册。您在使用本应用时产生的全部数据（药品名称、服用计划、服药打卡记录、应用设置）均仅保存在您的设备本地，不会上传至任何服务器。

1.2 自动收集的信息

无。本应用不自动收集任何设备信息或个人信息，不集成任何统计、广告或崩溃收集第三方 SDK。

1.3 我们不会收集的信息

本应用不收集以下信息：

  • 精确地理位置
  • 通讯录
  • 健康数据（不接入 HealthKit）
  • 设备标识符（IDFA / IDFV）
  • 崩溃日志与分析数据

2. 我们如何使用这些信息

  • 向您提供本应用的核心功能：服药提醒、服药记录与日历回顾
  • 以上全部处理均在您的设备本地完成

我们不会将您的个人信息用于上述目的之外的用途，也不会向第三方出售您的个人信息。

3. 第三方服务

本应用仅在您主动购买付费功能时使用以下系统服务：

  • StoreKit（Apple 内购系统）：由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理您的交易信息，我们不接触您的支付凭据

除此之外，本应用不集成任何第三方数据收集服务，无自建服务器。

4. 信息存储与安全

  • 存储地点：您的全部数据（药品计划与服药记录）仅存储在您的设备本地，无云端同步、无云端备份
  • 存储期限：数据保留至您卸载本应用或在本应用内删除对应内容为止
  • 安全措施：数据不出设备即是最主要的保护措施；卸载本应用将同时删除全部本地数据

5. 您的权利

您有权：

  • 访问和查看您的全部本地数据（本应用内直接展示）
  • 在本应用内随时删除任一药品及其记录
  • 卸载本应用以删除全部本地数据（本应用无账号体系，无远程留存）
  • 撤回您先前授予的权限（在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 到点吃药」中调整通知权限）

6. 未成年人保护

本应用的目标用户不包含未满 14 周岁的未成年人。如果您是未满 14 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人同意并陪同下使用本应用。我们不会在未获得监护人同意的情况下主动收集未成年人的个人信息。

7. 政策更新

我们可能会不定期更新本政策。更新后的政策将在本页面发布，并修改生效日期。重大变更我们会通过应用内通知或其他方式提示您。

English Version

wangzz (“we”, “us”, “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use DoseOnTime (the “App”), and the choices you have regarding that information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The App has no account system and requires no registration. All data you generate while using the App (medication names, schedule plans, dose confirmation records, and app settings) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

None. The App does not automatically collect any device or personal information, and integrates no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting third-party SDKs.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Health data (no HealthKit integration)
  • Device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • Crash logs and analytics data

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the core functionality of the App: medication reminders, dose records, and calendar review
  • All of this processing happens locally on your device

We do not use your personal information for purposes beyond those stated above, nor do we sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following system service only when you actively purchase paid features:

  • StoreKit (Apple In-App Purchase): Apple processes your transaction information under its own privacy policy; we never touch your payment credentials

Beyond this, the App integrates no third-party data collection services and has no backend server.

4. Storage and Security

  • Location: All your data (medication plans and dose records) is stored only on your device, with no cloud sync or cloud backup
  • Retention: Data is retained until you uninstall the App or delete the corresponding content in the App
  • Safeguards: Keeping data on-device is the primary protection; uninstalling the App deletes all local data

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review all your local data (displayed directly in the App)
  • Delete any medication and its records at any time within the App
  • Uninstall the App to delete all local data (the App has no account system and no remote retention)
  • Withdraw previously granted permissions (via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → DoseOnTime for notifications)

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 13, please use the App only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date. We will notify you of material changes through in-app notifications or other means.
