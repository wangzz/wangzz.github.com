中文版

wangzz（以下简称”我们”）高度重视您的隐私保护。本政策说明您在使用 ReviewImage（以下简称”本应用”）时，我们如何处理您的信息。本应用是一款帮助开发者将设备截图快速转换为 App Store Connect 所需规格展示图的本地工具，所有图像处理均在您的设备本地完成。请您在使用前仔细阅读。

1. 我们收集的信息

本应用完全在您的设备本地运行，不收集、不上传任何用户数据。

  • 您的截图与导出图片：仅在您自己的设备本地处理与保存，绝不会上传至任何服务器
  • 我们不要求您注册账号，也不收集您的姓名、邮箱、手机号等身份信息

1.1 我们不会收集的信息

本应用不会收集以下信息：

  • 您的精确地理位置
  • 您的通讯录
  • 您的健康与生物识别数据
  • 您的 Apple ID 或其他账号密码
  • 任何用于追踪目的的设备标识符（如 IDFA / IDFV）
  • 任何不必要的后台行为数据

2. 照片库权限

本应用仅在您主动点击「保存」时请求照片库写入权限（NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription），用于将导出的展示图保存到您的相册。我们不会读取您的相册内容，也不会在后台访问照片库。

3. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何需要收集用户信息的第三方服务。所有图像渲染（截图合成、渐变背景、设备边框、文字叠加、尺寸适配）均在设备本地通过 Apple 系统框架完成，不存在云端接口。

4. 信息存储与安全

  • 本地优先：您的所有截图与导出结果仅存储在您的设备本地，由您自主管理
  • 无云端：本应用没有任何对外网络请求，不会将您的图片或操作数据发送至外部服务器
  • 安全措施：图像处理在应用进程内完成，不写入任何外部对象存储；应用仅申请实现功能所必需的系统权限

5. 您的权利

您有权：

  • 随时删除设备本地的截图与导出结果
  • 在 iOS / macOS 系统设置中撤回已授予的照片库权限
  • 通过 wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com 联系开发者行使您的相关权利

6. 未成年人保护

本应用主要面向开发者与产品设计人员，不针对未满 14 周岁的未成年人设计。我们不会主动收集未成年人的个人信息。

English Version

wangzz (“we”, “us”, “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use ReviewImage (the “App”). The App is a local tool that helps developers quickly convert device screenshots into the sized preview images required by App Store Connect. All image processing is performed entirely on your device. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App runs completely on your device and does not collect or upload any user data.

  • Your screenshots and exported images: processed and saved only on your own device; never uploaded to any server
  • We do not require account registration and do not collect identity information such as your name, email, or phone number
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs

1.1 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts
  • Your health or biometric data
  • Your Apple ID or other account credentials
  • Any device identifiers (such as IDFA / IDFV) used for tracking purposes
  • Any unnecessary background behavioral data

2. Photo Library Permission

The App requests photo library write permission (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription) only when you actively tap “Save”, in order to store exported preview images to your photo album. We do not read your photo library contents and do not access the photo library in the background.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party service that collects user information. All image rendering (screenshot composition, gradient background, device frame, text overlay, size adaptation) is performed locally on device through Apple system frameworks; there is no cloud interface.

4. Storage and Security

  • Local-first: all your screenshots and exported results are stored only on your device and managed by you
  • No cloud: the App makes no outbound network requests and does not send your images or usage data to any external server
  • Safeguards: image processing happens within the app process and is never written to any external object storage; the App requests only the system permissions necessary for its functions

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete screenshots and exported results from your device at any time
  • Withdraw the granted photo library permission in iOS / macOS system settings
  • Contact the developer at wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com to exercise your rights

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for developers and product designers and is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date. We will notify you of material changes through in-app notifications or other means.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or complaints about this policy, please contact:

  • Developer: wangzz
  • Email: wzzvictory_tjsd@163.com
